Battle Clans Lane

Pre-release · Android · Unity 6 ECS · Optor Games

A new lane-battle strategy game: army orders, enemy AI and spell-casters, built on ECS for 60–240 units per battle. Sole developer. Gameplay from the current build.

ECS battle pipeline

30 Hz fixed tick with ordered phases (Sense, Decide, Act, Resolve); unit state model picked by benchmark; six architecture decision records.

No physics for crowds

Benchmarked Unity Physics against a sorted lane array: 240 units, 7.2 ms vs 0.41 ms per tick (editor, Burst), so units use a custom contact solver.

GPU-baked units

One-click baking of rigged units into GPU-animated ECS units; hit frames come from the baked clip events.

Effects

Wizard fireball, lightning, hammer impacts, castle damage and health bars, tuned live from a look-only VFX config.

Mobile build & startup

Cold-start benchmark (adb, Perfetto, boot marks); deferred ECS world and lobby loads; Android texture caps; fixed an IL2CPP-only crash on first spawn.

Testing & diagnostics

190+ EditMode tests, headless bot matches and a 52-match AI sweep; an always-on unit flight recorder logs anomalies.

More work

Sling Siege (physics-driven siege game: DOTS/ECS rework took maps with 2,000 connections from unplayable to playable), War Strategy of Battle Clans (deterministic ECS tick groups, PhysX catch-up capped), TrueFoundry Grand Prix (Editor tools for batch LODs, baked lighting) and more are in the portfolio PDF.

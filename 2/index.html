Battle Clans Lane

Pre-release · Android · Unity 6 ECS · Optor Games · sole developer

A new lane-battle strategy game: army orders, enemy AI and spell-casters, built on ECS for 60–240 units per battle. Gameplay from the current build.

Everything here is Unity and C#. Gameplay structure, AI, game feel and profiling are the parts I'd bring to an Unreal team, and I'm ready to ramp up on Unreal and C++.

Effects

Wizard fireball, lightning, hammer impacts, castle damage and health bars; fireball, hammer and health bars tuned live from a look-only VFX config.

Hits on the impact frame

Rigged units baked in one click into GPU-animated ECS units; gameplay owns the timing, and hits land on the animation's impact frame from the baked clip events.

ECS battle pipeline

30 Hz fixed tick with ordered phases (Sense, Decide, Act, Resolve); same-seed replays checked by state hash in tests; six architecture decision records.

Testing & diagnostics

190+ EditMode tests, headless bot matches and a 52-match AI sweep across decks, opponents and seeds; a development-build flight recorder logs unit anomalies.

No physics for crowds

Benchmarked Unity Physics against a sorted lane array: 240 units, 7.2 ms vs 0.41 ms per tick for the lane alone (editor benchmark, Burst). Units now use the sorted lane with a light contact pass.
